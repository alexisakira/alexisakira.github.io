Good news. [*Econ Journal Watch*](https://econjwatch.org/) now officially accepts LaTeX submissions. Its [Instructions for Authors & Style Guide](https://econjwatch.org/call-for-papers/instructions-for-authors-style-guide) states that authors may submit manuscripts in LaTeX using the downloadable template.

EJW has long been one of my favorite journals, but it had not accepted LaTeX submissions. As I wrote in a [previous post](/posts/2025/09/blog-post-30/), I had been urging the editorial board to make the transition. In 2025, EJW began publishing papers typeset in LaTeX, but the author-facing submission process had not yet been formally established.

I volunteered to develop the EJW LaTeX class file and presented it in my talk, ["A Public LaTeX Template for Econ Journal Watch"](/talks/), at the online EJW Powwow on August 23. The class file is now accepted by EJW and available through its official author instructions. It is also available as an [Overleaf template](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/manuscript-template-for-econ-journal-watch-ejw/xxvbryjywqhh), so authors can begin writing in the browser without any local setup.

I am proud to have played a part in this development. I hope that the template makes it easier for authors to submit clear, consistently formatted papers to a journal that I greatly admire.
